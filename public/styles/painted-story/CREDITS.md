# Painted Story · demo images

All images in this folder were generated for this kit's own films from our own prompts (no third-party artwork, no
photos of real people), downscaled for the demo. You may use them in your own videos.

| folder | what | made with |
|---|---|---|
| `wide/` (s002, s006, s019, s026, s032, s034, s036, s039, s040, s047, i01, i02, i03, i05, i07) | 16:9 stills of the stick-figure character in his flat (1600 px) | Gemini app · Nano Banana (image generation) |
| `tall/` (P16–P25) | 2:3 painterly plates of a quarantine ward, no character (1067×1600) | ChatGPT image generation |
| `tall/` (Y07, Y09, Y11, S23) | transparent cut-outs: the hero sitting, the hand writing, the hero in bed, a figure in a hazmat suit | ChatGPT image generation (transparent PNG → WebP) |

The prompts that made images like these are in `src/styles/painted-story/pipeline/prompts.*.example.json`.
